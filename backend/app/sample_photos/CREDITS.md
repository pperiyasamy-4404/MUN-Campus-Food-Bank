# Sample food photos

Used only by the sample data. The food bank can replace any of them with its own
photo on the Stock page.

Every photo is free to use. Some are in the public domain (CC0, or marked public
domain): anyone may use them for anything. The others are CC BY: anyone may use
them if the photographer is credited, which this file does. All were found through
Openverse (openverse.org) or Wikimedia Commons, then cropped to 4:3 and shrunk to
640x480 (that is the only change made to them).

Some canned food shows a can, and some shows the food itself. There is no free
photo of a can of chicken, so canned-chicken.jpg was made from two CC0 photos:
the opened tins from the tomato photo, filled with the shredded chicken. That is
the one photo changed beyond cropping and shrinking; CC0 allows it. Five photos were swapped for clearer ones
on 13 Nov (pasta sauce, coffee, instant noodles, canned chicken, canned tomatoes).
